Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account you can practise every task – but progress isn't saved and is gone once you close the app. With an account it's kept, including on a new device.

I forgot my password.

Tap “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen. You'll receive an email with a link to reset it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap Account at the top right, then Delete account permanently. After a confirmation prompt, your account and all practice data are deleted immediately and irrevocably.

How is the difficulty chosen?

Two correct answers in a row raise the level by one, two wrong ones lower it by one – separately for each module. You can switch to manual at any time and pick the level yourself.

Why do I sometimes get the same task?

Every task is generated from a starting value (a “seed”). The same seed and the same level deliberately produce the same task – that's what makes mock exams repeatable.

Are these real aptitude test questions?

No. All tasks are generated by the app and follow common test formats. They are not taken from the selection procedures of any particular employer.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your device language. To override it, use the language buttons on the sign-in screen or under Account.
