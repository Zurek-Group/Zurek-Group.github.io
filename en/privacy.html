3. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no advertising IDs.
  • No third-party analytics services. Usage events are evaluated exclusively in our own database and passed on to nobody. Only fixed identifiers such as “module opened” are recorded – never task content, never your answers, never free text.
  • No device identifier, no IP address, no location – neither in the usage events nor in the four counters.
  • No recognition without an account. Anyone using the app without signing in receives no identifier – not an anonymous one, not a temporary one. What we know about this case is counts and nothing else.
  • No passing on or selling of your data to third parties.
  • No profiling for purposes outside the app; the competence profile serves only to steer your own practice.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No automated decisions with legal effect within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The app does adapt the difficulty automatically to your results – but that only decides which task comes next, nothing else.
  • The app is aimed at people aged 16 and over preparing for a selection procedure. We do not knowingly collect data from children; purchases run through the Apple ID and its age rules.

3a. What stays stored on your device

The app stores on the device only what it needs to run – your account session, your language and appearance choice, goal and career, the subscription status, your aptitude test results, the time and days of your reminder, how many trial tasks per Premium area have been used, which milestones have already been shown, whether you were asked for a rating, the markers for the four counters, and a queue of practice data not yet transmitted. None of it is read to recognise you outside the app, and none of it is advertising or tracking. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German implementation of the ePrivacy Directive); no consent is therefore required. Deleting the app removes all of it.

3b. Reminders

If you switch on a reminder in the app, iOS asks once for permission to show notifications. The app schedules reminders only on your device (local notifications): no push service is involved, and neither the time nor the text is transmitted to us or anyone else. You can switch them off in the app at any time or withdraw the permission in the iOS settings.

4. Processors

For accounts and storage we use Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA; Postgres database and authentication). The servers are located in the Frankfurt am Main region, Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider; should Supabase access data from the USA for support or maintenance, the EU standard contractual clauses apply.

The app's emails – address confirmation, password reset, notices about changes to the account – are sent on our behalf by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France). For this Brevo receives your email address and the content of the respective email and processes them on servers in the EU; a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place. We do not send marketing emails.

If you use Sign in with Apple, data is exchanged between your device, Apple and us. Apple acts as an independent controller (for users in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Cork, Ireland); what data Apple itself processes is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

The app is distributed, and the Premium subscription billed, through Apple's App Store. What data Apple collects in doing so – such as your payment details – is governed by Apple's own privacy policy; we have no influence over it and do not receive that data.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the account and all practice data are deleted immediately and irrevocably – including the usage events, the subscription confirmation and, if present, the Apple token. The subscription itself does not end with that: it exists with Apple and continues until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

One exception that cannot be avoided: the four counters without personal reference from the table above remain when an account is deleted. They carry no identifier – so there is nothing by which they could be attributed to your account, and therefore nothing by which they could be deleted individually. That is precisely the price of collecting them without any personal reference at all. The database deletes them automatically once they are older than 400 days. They reveal only how many people have tried the app in total, never who.

6. Deleting your account

Deletion is possible directly in the app: Account → Delete account permanently. No contact with support is needed. Alternatively, an informal email to the address above is enough.

If you signed in with Apple, we first revoke the link with Apple and only then delete your account with all practice data. The token stored for this purpose is deleted along with it. As a result, the app also disappears from your Apple settings under “Sign in with Apple”.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20 GDPR). To exercise them, contact the address above; we reply within one month.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of a legitimate interest – the usage events, the technical connection data and the crash reports – you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. An informal email to the address above is enough. We will then stop processing the data concerned unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority – for example the one responsible for us: Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und für das Recht auf Akteneinsicht Brandenburg, Stahnsdorfer Damm 77, 14532 Kleinmachnow, Germany, lda.brandenburg.de. We have not appointed a data protection officer; the statutory thresholds are not met.
