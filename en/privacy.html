Signing in with Apple is optional. The route via email and password remains available as an equal alternative. If you sign in with Apple, we store no password of yours.

The Premium subscription is concluded with Apple, not with us: the purchase runs through your Apple ID, Apple bills it, and it is cancelled in your Apple ID settings. The app only receives Apple's signed confirmation described in the table above. Without an account the subscription stays with your Apple ID; with an account we also record its status there, so Premium applies after a reinstall or on a second device without any detour.

Without an account, everything you do stays on the device: progress lives only in memory and is gone once you close the app. Nothing is stored that could later be attributed to anyone.

In that case, the only data transmitted are the four counters from the table above – each at most once, without any identifier and without content. They show how many people try the app, but not who, and not whether two counters came from the same device.

3. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no advertising IDs.
  • No third-party analytics services. Usage events are evaluated exclusively in our own database and passed on to nobody. Only fixed identifiers such as “module opened” are recorded – never task content, never your answers, never free text.
  • No device identifier, no IP address, no location – neither in the usage events nor in the four counters.
  • No recognition without an account. Anyone using the app without signing in receives no identifier – not an anonymous one, not a temporary one. What we know about this case is counts and nothing else.
  • No passing on or selling of your data to third parties.
  • No profiling for purposes outside the app; the competence profile serves only to steer your own practice.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

4. Processors

For accounts and storage we use Supabase (Postgres database and authentication). The servers are located in the Frankfurt am Main region, Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

If you use Sign in with Apple, data is exchanged between your device, Apple and us. What data Apple itself processes is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

The app is distributed, and the Premium subscription billed, through Apple's App Store. What data Apple collects in doing so – such as your payment details – is governed by Apple's own privacy policy; we have no influence over it and do not receive that data.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the account and all practice data are deleted immediately and irrevocably – including the usage events, the subscription confirmation and, if present, the Apple token. The subscription itself does not end with that: it exists with Apple and continues until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

One exception that cannot be avoided: the four counters without personal reference from the table above remain. They carry no identifier – so there is nothing by which they could be attributed to your account, and therefore nothing by which they could be deleted. That is precisely the price of collecting them without any personal reference at all. They reveal only how many people have tried the app in total, never who.

6. Deleting your account

Deletion is possible directly in the app: Account → Delete account permanently. No contact with support is needed. Alternatively, an informal email to the address above is enough.

If you signed in with Apple, we first revoke the link with Apple and only then delete your account with all practice data. The token stored for this purpose is deleted along with it. As a result, the app also disappears from your Apple settings under “Sign in with Apple”.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). To exercise them, contact the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

If the data processing changes, we update this policy and adjust the date above.
