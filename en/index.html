GET READY

Brain training and preparation for aptitude tests – two minutes on the train, or structured practice before the exam.

What the app does

  • Rule of three – proportional, inversely proportional and compound, each with a full worked solution
  • Number sequences – seven kinds of rule, from arithmetic sequences to Fibonacci
  • Word analogies – fourteen kinds of relationship, from synonyms to collective terms
  • Situational judgement – workplace situations, evaluated by behavioural traits
  • Schulte tables – concentration and visual search speed

The difficulty adapts on its own: two correct answers in a row raise the level, two wrong ones lower it.

The app is available in English and German and follows your device language.
